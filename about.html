What We Do

End-to-End Revenue Cycle Management Tailored to Your Practice

RevBridge LLC delivers complete revenue cycle management, handling eligibility verification, prior authorization support, charge entry, claim scrubbing, payment posting, denial management, A/R follow-up, and patient billing.

Every stage of your financial cycle is owned by one accountable team. We work directly inside the EHR and practice management software you already use, including eClinicalWorks (eCW), CollaborateMD, athenahealth, AdvancedMD, WebPT, Tebra, ClaimMD, Practice Suite, DrChrono, CureMD, Nextech, CareCloud, and others—so onboarding never requires changing systems or disrupting your staff.

Zero Software Changes

We integrate directly into your existing practice management tools, ensuring seamless setup with no software migrations or steep learning curves.

Full-Cycle Accountability

From pre-authorization to final patient collection, one dedicated team takes full responsibility for driving maximum reimbursement.

Signed BAA & Compliance

We execute formal Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and ensure full HIPAA regulatory compliance across all billing workflows.

Unaddressed claim denials, missed value-based coding (CCM/AWV/TCM), and unhelpful financial reporting leave practice leaders in the dark.
