Understanding the Basics

What Is Revenue Cycle Management?

Revenue cycle management (RCM) follows the entire financial journey of a patient visit. It starts with eligibility checks and provider enrollment, then moves through claims, denials, A/R follow-up, and reporting.

Medical billing, the service described on this page, is the claims portion of that larger cycle. Here is how the stages connect:

  • Eligibility and enrollment: confirming coverage and payer participation before care is delivered
  • Claims: accurate charge entry and clean claim submission
  • Denials and A/R follow-up: working rejected and unpaid claims until they resolve
  • Reporting: clear numbers on collections, denials, and payer trends

Our Process

RevBridge LLC's End-to-End RCM Process

Step 01

Claim Submission

Timely, accurate claims are the base of a healthy revenue cycle. Our billing specialists check patient demographics, confirm insurance eligibility, and send out clean claims for you. Quality checks catch errors before submission, which reduces the rejections and delays that cost practices money.

Step 02

Denial Management

Even clean claims can be denied. Our team reviews each rejected claim, finds the root cause, and files appeals promptly to recover revenue that might otherwise be written off. We also watch denial patterns across your payers and adjust submission practices to prevent repeat issues.

Step 03

Payment Posting

When payments come in, we post them against the right open claims and confirm each deposit reaches your practice's bank account. Accurate posting avoids manual reconciliation errors and leaves a clear audit trail for your accounting team.

Step 04

Reporting & Analytics

You can't improve what you don't measure. Our reports cover collections performance, denial rates, A/R aging, and payer-level trends, giving administrators and physicians the visibility they need to make confident financial decisions.

What is the difference between medical billing and revenue cycle management?

Revenue cycle management (RCM) covers the whole financial path of a patient visit, from eligibility checks and provider enrollment through claims, denials, A/R follow-up, and reporting. Medical billing is the claims portion of that cycle. If you want us to run the full cycle, credentialing included, our RCM services are the better fit.
