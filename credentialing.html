Our Credentialing Process

Comprehensive Credentialing & Provider Enrollment

Provider Credentialing

Credentialing is the verification step. We confirm each clinician's education, licensure, work history, malpractice coverage, NPI details, and other qualifications so payers have a complete, accurate record.

Provider Enrollment

Enrollment puts that verified data to work. We apply with each payer so your provider or group can join its network and get reimbursed for the care you deliver.

RevBridge LLC manages both sides of the workflow. We prepare applications, review CAQH and payer profiles, submit enrollment packets, and follow up with payer representatives until your participation is confirmed.

Our team handles credentialing across Medicare, Medicaid, BCBS, and commercial carriers. We support provider enrollment for individual and group NPIs, check each application for readiness, keep your credentialing records current, and follow every payer's specific rules so claims are billed under the right participation status. We also keep you ahead of renewals and recredentialing as payer networks, ownership structures, and documentation requirements change.

F.A.Q

Credentialing Questions, Answered

What is the difference between credentialing and provider enrollment?

Credentialing is the verification step, where we confirm a provider's education, licensure, work history, malpractice coverage, and certifications. Enrollment uses that verified data to apply with each payer so the provider can join the network and bill for care. RevBridge LLC handles both, from verifying credentials to following up with every payer until your participation is confirmed.

Which payers can you help with?

We handle Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and commercial carriers. That includes individual and group NPI enrollment, hospital and facility privileging, and coordination with your billing team so claims go out under the correct participation status.

Can you handle Medicare and Medicaid enrollment?

Yes. We manage initial applications, documentation, and follow-up with your Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) or state Medicaid agency. We also take care of PECOS updates and revalidations so your enrollment stays active.

How long does credentialing take?

It depends on the payer, the provider's specialty, and how complete the documentation is. Most applications take anywhere from several weeks to a few months, and many land in the 60-120 day range. We can't promise exact approval dates, but we shorten timelines by submitting clean applications and following up consistently.

What information do I need to get started?

You'll typically need your NPI details, active licenses and DEA registration, education and training history, work history, malpractice coverage details, CAQH login information, and your group NPI and tax ID details. We give you a complete checklist at the start so nothing gets missed.

What happens to my revenue while credentialing is in progress?

If a provider sees patients before enrollment is confirmed, claims can be denied or paid at out-of-network rates. We help you plan start dates around the enrollment timeline and track every application to minimize gaps. Depending on the payer, retroactive billing may be possible once enrollment is approved, and we'll advise you on the specifics.

Can you manage credentialing for multiple locations or a large group?

Yes. We keep a single master file for every provider, link providers to your group contracts and tax ID, and file location changes with every payer. Expirations and recredentialing dates are tracked in one calendar, so nothing lapses across sites.
